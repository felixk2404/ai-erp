# AI Electronics ERP — film design system

Mood: night console. OLED-black glass, graphite panels, one electric-cyan signal, LED dots, mono readouts. Cinematic, not web.

## Colors
- void #0d1017 (canvas) · chassis #161a23 · chassis-2 #1e2330 · chassis-3 #252b3a
- readout #f3f5f9 · readout-2 #bcc4d1 · readout-3 #94a0b2
- signal #5ad1ff · signal-hover #8ee0ff · signal-glow rgba(90,209,255,.45) · signal-soft rgba(90,209,255,.12)
- led-green #34d17a · led-amber #f0b429 (human task) · led-red #ff5a5f
- rule rgba(255,255,255,.10) · rule-strong rgba(255,255,255,.20)

## Type
- Titles: Heebo 800, tracking -0.02em, 72–140px.
- Body: IBM Plex Sans 500, 32–44px.
- Data: IBM Plex Mono 500, 24–40px, cyan or readout-2.
- Captions: IBM Plex Sans 500 36px readout on a void/80 scrim bar, bottom-center, max 2 lines. Caption zone = bottom 160px, never covered.

## Do
Cyan glow on anything "live". Amber only for things a human must do. Real captures framed in a chassis "device" with a 2px rule and inset glow. Ambient drift on decoratives. Big scale pops on entrances allowed (exaggerated register approved).

## Don't
No other accent hues. No particles, glitch or lens flares. No text under 24px. No mocked UI, only real captures.
